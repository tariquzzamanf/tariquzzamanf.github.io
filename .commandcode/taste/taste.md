# Taste

## Workflow

- Maintains the CV in two parallel representations and expects edits mirrored across all of them: the LaTeX source (`files/cv/tariq.tex`) plus its recompiled PDF, and the web CV page (`content/cv.html` + regenerated site). When adding/updating CV entries, apply them to both the LaTeX and web versions rather than one. Confidence: 0.7

## Content

- Wants notable achievements recorded in the dedicated "Awards & Achievements" section, not only as inline details under an education/career entry, listed reverse-chronologically. Confidence: 0.5
