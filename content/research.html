Misinformation & harmful content

These projects ask how harmful and misleading text can be detected in the conditions where it actually circulates: informal, code-mixed, and in languages with little labelled data.

Findings of ACL 2026

Same Claim, Different Judgment: Benchmarking Scenario-Induced Bias in Multilingual Financial Misinformation Detection

A benchmark for financial misinformation detection across English, Chinese, Greek, and Bengali, built to test whether a verdict on the same claim holds up across languages.

LLM evaluation & bias

A detector is only as trustworthy as the model behind it. This work asks whether large language models return the same judgment when the language, the setting, or the person asking changes.

Findings of ACL 2026

Scenario-Induced Bias in Multilingual Model Judgments

MFMD-Scen evaluates 22 models across four languages. It holds the financial claim fixed while varying financial personas, regional contexts, and cultural identities, isolating how much of a verdict comes from the scenario rather than the claim.

Low-resource & Bangla NLP

Bangla is spoken by more than 230 million people, yet informal and in-domain labelled data remains scarce. These projects build the representations and data practices needed to work with the language people actually write.

Workshop paper · BLP @ EMNLP 2023 · Best Shared Task Paper Award

A Novel Informal Bangla FastText Embedding for Violence Inciting Text Detection

A custom FastText embedding trained on informal Bangla social media text, paired with a BiLSTM for violence-inciting text detection under noisy, code-mixed conditions.

Preprint · arXiv 2024

BDA: Bangla Text Data Augmentation Framework

A framework combining synonym replacement, random swap, back-translation, and paraphrasing with semantic filtering, helping small labelled Bangla datasets reach near full-data performance.

Accessibility & sign language

Sign languages are low-resource twice over: there is little data, and little instructional material. This work focuses on generating usable written guidance for learners of Bangla Sign Language.

Workshop paper · CV4A11y at ICCV 2025

Prompting with Sign Parameters for Low-resource Sign Language Instruction Generation

A prompting method that conditions on handshape, orientation, location, and movement to generate sign-language instructions, released alongside the BdSLIG dataset.
